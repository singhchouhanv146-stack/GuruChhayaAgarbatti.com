About Us

Guru Chhaya Agarbatti & Marketing is a trusted and growing name in the field of agarbatti manufacturing and distribution, based in Jai Prakash Nagar, Adhartal, Jabalpur (Madhya Pradesh). With a strong commitment to quality, affordability, and customer satisfaction, our company is engaged in the production of a wide range of agarbatti products, including scented agarbatti, daily use agarbatti, premium fragrance agarbatti, and mosquito repellent agarbatti, along with dhoop products used for puja and spiritual purposes.


Our products are designed to create a pleasant and peaceful environment, suitable for homes, temples, and commercial spaces. Each product is carefully prepared using selected raw materials to ensure long-lasting fragrance and smooth burning. We continuously work on improving our product quality and introducing new fragrances to meet changing customer preferences.


In addition to manufacturing, we also deal in agarbatti making machines and cotton wick machines. We support individuals, small businesses, and startups who want to begin their own agarbatti production work. Our machines are easy to operate, cost-effective, and suitable for both small-scale and growing businesses. We also guide our customers on how to start and manage production effectively.


Guru Chhaya Agarbatti & Marketing serves a wide range of clients including local customers, shopkeepers, wholesalers, and distributors. Our strong local network helps us deliver products quickly and maintain long-term relationships based on trust and reliability. We offer products in a wide price range from ₹5 per piece to ₹400 per kilogram, making them suitable for every type of customer.


We are also committed to supporting employment in the local area. Our company regularly provides job opportunities for machine operators, packaging workers, and laborers. We offer a simple and supportive work environment where both skilled and unskilled workers can easily learn and earn. This helps many families generate income and improve their living conditions.


Our vision is to grow as a reliable and recognized brand in the agarbatti industry while maintaining our core values of honesty, quality, and customer satisfaction. We aim to expand our reach, improve our services, and continue contributing to local business development and employment generation.


Guru Chhaya Agarbatti & Marketing believes in building strong relationships with customers, workers, and business partners. We are dedicated to providing the best products, fair pricing, and excellent service. Our focus is not only on business growth but also on creating value for the community and supporting people who depend on this industry.
